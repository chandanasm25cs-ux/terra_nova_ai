// ABOUT THE PROJECT

What is TerraNova AI?

AI-Based Disaster Damage Assessment System

TerraNova AI is an interdisciplinary project developed at RNSIT that harnesses Artificial Intelligence to assess and respond to natural disasters in real time.

Focusing on floods and earthquakes, our platform enables government officials, NGOs, and citizens to report damage, access AI-generated insights, and coordinate emergency response efficiently.

// KEY INFORMATION

Why This Matters

🌊

FLOOD IMPACT

Floods are the most frequent natural disaster globally, causing over $40 billion in damages annually and displacing millions of people from their homes every year.

🌍

SEISMIC ACTIVITY

Over 1.4 million earthquakes are recorded each year. Major quakes can destroy entire cities within seconds, making rapid damage assessment critically essential.

🤖

AI ADVANTAGE

AI-based assessment reduces manual survey time from weeks to hours, enabling faster resource allocation, rescue coordination, and lives saved in critical windows.

📡

REAL-TIME DATA

Integration with satellite imagery, IoT sensors, and citizen reports creates a comprehensive real-time picture of disaster zones for decision makers.

🏛️

GOVERNMENT READY

Designed for government officials, NGOs, emergency responders, and citizens — our platform bridges the gap between data and decisive action.

🛡️

PATENT POTENTIAL

Developed at RNSIT with innovation at its core, TerraNova AI is built to stand out, scale, and potentially qualify for institutional patent recognition.

How TerraNova AI Helps

01📲

CITIZEN REPORTING

Anyone can submit disaster reports with photos, location, and damage details from any device in real time.

02🧠

AI DAMAGE SCORING

Our AI model analyzes submitted data and imagery to generate automated severity and damage scores.

03🗺️

GEOSPATIAL MAPPING

Affected areas are mapped in real time, giving officials a clear visual of the disaster footprint and priorities.

04🚨

EMERGENCY ALERTS

Automated alerts are dispatched to relevant authorities and NGOs based on report severity and location.

05📊

ANALYTICS DASHBOARD

Role-based dashboards give each user type exactly the data they need to act swiftly.

06💬

RESOURCE COORDINATION

Request rescue teams, medical aid, or supplies and track response status in real time.
